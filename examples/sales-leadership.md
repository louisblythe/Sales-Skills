# Sales leadership examples

Each example uses fictional data pasted into the conversation.

## Sales Manager

**Prompt.** Ask `sales-skills:sales-manager` for a coaching review. Mia marked a deal as commit after sending a proposal. The buyer has confirmed the problem and agreed to a Thursday call. Budget and approval authority are unknown.

**Example output.** Buyer evidence supports an active evaluation. Commit is the rep’s assessment. Mia should use Thursday’s call to confirm who approves the purchase, whether funding is available, and when a decision is expected. Review the forecast after that call.

## Head of Sales

**Prompt.** Ask `sales-skills:head-of-sales` for an executive brief. Quarterly target is AUD 400,000, closed won is AUD 160,000, open commit is AUD 140,000, and separate open upside is AUD 70,000. These categories do not overlap.

**Example output.** Closed won plus open commit yields an AUD 300,000 forecast, AUD 100,000 below target. If all identified upside closes, the forecast reaches AUD 370,000 and remains AUD 30,000 short. Ask segment owners to confirm buyer decision dates and identify where the remaining gap could be covered.

## Sales Reporting

**Prompt.** Ask `sales-skills:sales-reporting` for May funnel conversion. The May cohort has 40 qualified opportunities. By month end, 10 reached proposal and 4 of those were won. No previous-month figures were provided.

| Metric | Calculation | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified to proposal | 10 ÷ 40 | 25% |
| Proposal to won | 4 ÷ 10 | 40% |
| Qualified to won | 4 ÷ 40 | 10% |
| Month-over-month change | Previous-month cohort missing | Unknown |

These are month-end rates for the May cohort. Opportunities still open may change the final conversion rates.
